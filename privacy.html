Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

The short version. Almost everything you type into this site — your PRs and training log — never leaves your own device. What is sent to a server is your email, password, and chosen username when you make an account, plus whatever you type into the AI features (coach messages, workout-generator settings and notes, visualizer prompts, 3D-reconstruction cues). Nothing is sold, and there is no advertising or tracking.

1. Who runs this site

Tidelyne is an independent educational project operated by Scarpline. It is run by an individual competitive swimmer, not a company with staff or investors. Contact: danielswims101@gmail.com

2. What is stored on your device only

These use your browser's local storage. They are never transmitted to us, and we cannot see them. Clearing your browser data deletes them permanently, which is why the My PRs page offers an Export Backup button (and an Import to restore it on another device).

  • Your swimmer name and personal records
  • Your training log entries (the weekly totals and charts on the Analytics page are computed from them each time and are not stored)
  • Your Race Planner goals and meet dates, timer settings, analytics chart preferences, recent searches, the last release you read on the What's New page, and small interface preferences such as the search shortcut
  • If you log in, your sign-in session (a token, not your password) is kept in the browser so you stay logged in; Log out removes it

4. Processors we use

  • Supabase — accounts, authentication, and the AI usage counter.
  • Anthropic — processes the text you send to the AI features to generate replies.
  • Resend — delivers the single signup-notification email to the site owner.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts the site files; server logs may include IP addresses.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) — technique videos are embedded through youtube-nocookie.com, so YouTube does not set tracking cookies unless you press play on a video. Video thumbnails are loaded from YouTube's image servers (i.ytimg.com) only when the Videos page (or the stroke-demo strip on the 3D Visualizer page) is open, and the full YouTube player — with its scripts and any cookies — loads only after you press play on a video.
  • Google Fonts / jsDelivr — deliver fonts, the sign-in library and the 3D engine.

We do not use analytics, advertising networks, or third-party tracking pixels.

5. Please do not send us health information

Do not type medical details, injuries, diagnoses, or anything you would not want stored into any of the AI features. They are general coaching tools, not a confidential medical service.

6. Children

Accounts are for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete the account and its data promptly. Swimmers under 13 are welcome to use every part of the site that does not require an account — all the technique, calculator, log, and 3D content works without signing in — only the AI features (the coach, the workout generator, the visualizer's AI analysis, and AI 3D reconstruction) need an account.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, or delete the data associated with your account at any time by emailing danielswims101@gmail.com. Because on-device data never reaches us, you delete that yourself by clearing your browser data or using the Clear buttons in the app. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA; we honor those requests regardless of location.

8. Retention

Account records are kept until you ask us to delete them. The AI usage counter stores only the time of each request; rows older than the 5-hour window are no longer used but are kept with the account and deleted with it.

9. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change. Please check back periodically.
